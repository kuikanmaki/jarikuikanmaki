**_Update 2.15.21 – Newly surfaced Daszak email: “ [No need for you to sign the ‘Statement’ Ralph!!](https://usrtk.org/biohazards-blog/no-need-for-you-to-sign-the-statement-ralph/) ”_**

Emails obtained by U.S. Right to Know show that a [statement in _The Lancet_](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(20)30418-9/fulltext) authored by 27 prominent public health scientists condemning “conspiracy theories suggesting that COVID-19 does not have a natural origin” was organized by employees of EcoHealth Alliance, a non-profit group that has [received millions of dollars](https://www.usaspending.gov/keyword_search/%22ecohealth%20alliance%22) of [U.S. taxpayer](https://grantome.com/grant/NIH/R01-AI110964-04) funding to [genetically manipulate](https://journals.plos.org/plospathogens/article?id=10.1371/journal.ppat.1006698) [coronaviruses](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature12711) with scientists at the [Wuhan Institute of Virology](https://www.wsj.com/articles/chinas-bats-expert-says-her-wuhan-lab-wasnt-source-of-new-coronavirus-11587463204).

The emails obtained via public records requests show that EcoHealth Alliance President Peter Daszak drafted the _Lancet_ statement, and that he intended it to [“not be identifiable as coming from any one organization or person”](https://usrtk.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/The_Lancet_Emails_Daszak-2.6.20.pdf) but rather to be seen as [“simply a letter from leading scientists”](https://usrtk.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/The_Lancet_Emails_Daszak-2.8.20.pdf). Daszak wrote that he wanted “ [to avoid the appearance of a political statement](https://usrtk.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/The_Lancet_Emails_Daszak-2.8.20.pdf) ”.

The scientists’ letter appeared in _The Lancet_ on February 18, just one week after the World Health Organization announced that the disease caused by the novel coronavirus would be named COVID-19.

The 27 authors “strongly condemn\[ed\] conspiracy theories suggesting that COVID-19 does not have a natural origin,” and reported that scientists from multiple countries “overwhelmingly conclude that this coronavirus originated in wildlife.” The letter included no scientific references to refute a lab-origin theory of the virus. One scientist, Linda Saif, [asked via email whether it would be useful](https://usrtk.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/The_Lancet_Emails_Saif-2.6.20.pdf) “to add just one or 2 statements in support of why nCOV is not a lab generated virus and is naturally occuring? Seems critical to scientifically refute such claims!” Daszak responded, “ [I think we should probably stick to a broad statement](https://usrtk.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/The_Lancet_Emails_Daszak-2.6.20.pdf).”

[Growing calls](https://www.wsj.com/articles/so-where-did-the-virus-come-from-11590756909) to investigate the Wuhan Institute of Virology as a potential source of SARS-CoV-2 have led to [increased scrutiny](https://www.wsj.com/articles/nih-presses-u-s-nonprofit-for-information-on-wuhan-virology-lab-11597829400) of EcoHealth Alliance. The emails show how members of EcoHealth Alliance played an early role in framing questions about possible lab origin of SARS-CoV-2 as “crackpot theories that need to be addressed,” as [Daszak told _The Guardian_](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2020/feb/20/coronavirus-chinese-scientists-false-rumours-experts).

Although the phrase “EcoHealth Alliance” appeared only once in _The Lancet_ statement, in association with co-author Daszak, several other co-authors also have direct ties to the group that were not disclosed as conflicts of interest. Rita Colwell and James Hughes are [members](https://www.ecohealthalliance.org/board-of-directors) of the Board of Directors of EcoHealth Alliance, [William Karesh](https://www.ecohealthalliance.org/personnel/dr-william-karesh) is the group’s Executive Vice President for Health and Policy, and [Hume Field](https://www.ecohealthalliance.org/personnel/dr-hume-field) is Science and Policy Advisor.

The statement’s authors also claimed that the “rapid, open, and transparent sharing of data on this outbreak is now being threatened by rumours and misinformation around its origins.” Today, however, [little is known](https://www.washingtonpost.com/opinions/global-opinions/the-coronaviruss-origins-are-still-a-mystery-we-need-a-full-investigation/2020/11/13/cbf4390e-2450-11eb-8672-c281c7a2c96e_story.html) [about the origins](https://www.pnas.org/content/pnas/early/2020/11/03/2021133117.full.pdf) of SARS-CoV-2, and investigations into its origins by [the World Health Organization](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/coronaviruse/20200802-tors-chn-and-who-agreed-final-version.pdf?sfvrsn=5323728d_2&download=true) and [_The Lancet_ COVID-19 commission](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(20)31494-X/fulltext?dgcid=etoc-edschoice_email_tlcovid-1920&utm_source=SDSN&utm_campaign=c2a1003e78-EMAIL_CAMPAIGN_2018_03_02_COPY_02&utm_medium=email&utm_term=0_2302100059-c2a1003e78-178982095&mc_cid=c2a1003e78&mc_eid=a5648176e0) have been [shrouded in secrecy](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/11/02/world/who-china-coronavirus.html?smid=tw-share) and mired by [conflicts of interests](https://covid19commission.org/peter-daszak).

Peter Daszak, Rita Colwell, and _The Lancet_ Editor Richard Horton did not provide comments in response to our requests for this story.
