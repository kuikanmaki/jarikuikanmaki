WASHINGTON—The U.S. intelligence community publicly confirmed it is trying to determine whether [the coronavirus](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/coronavirus?mod=article_inline) may have escaped from a laboratory in Wuhan, the city where the pandemic began.

In an unusual public statement, the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, or ODNI, also said that U.S. intelligence agencies concur with the broad scientific consensus that “the Covid-19 virus was not manmade or genetically modified.”
