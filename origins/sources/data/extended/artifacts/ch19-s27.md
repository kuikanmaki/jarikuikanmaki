WASHINGTON—The U.S. Energy Department has concluded that the [Covid pandemic](https://www.wsj.com/articles/u-s-covid-19-pandemic-enters-fourth-year-with-hospitalizations-on-the-decline-11674185739?mod=article_inline) most likely [arose from a laboratory leak](https://www.wsj.com/articles/china-rejects-u-s-assertion-that-covid-originated-from-lab-leak-fe15df0c?mod=article_inline), according to a classified intelligence report recently provided to the White House and key members of Congress.

The shift by the Energy Department, which previously was undecided on [how the virus emerged](https://www.wsj.com/articles/covid-19-most-likely-leaked-from-lab-in-china-senate-gop-report-says-11666905063?mod=article_inline), is noted in an update to a 2021 document by Director of National Intelligence Avril Haines’s office.

The new report highlights how different parts of the intelligence community have arrived at disparate judgments about [the pandemic’s origin](https://www.wsj.com/articles/house-republicans-probe-covid-19-origins-government-response-11675290011?mod=article_inline). The Energy Department now [joins the Federal Bureau of Investigation](https://www.wsj.com/articles/fbi-director-says-covid-pandemic-likely-caused-by-chinese-lab-leak-13a5e69b?mod=article_inline) in saying the virus likely spread via a mishap at a Chinese laboratory. Four other agencies, along with a national intelligence panel, still judge that it was likely the result of [a natural transmission](https://www.wsj.com/articles/new-reconstruction-points-to-animal-origins-for-covid-19-11637262041?mod=article_inline), and two are undecided.

The Energy Department’s conclusion is the result of new intelligence and is significant because the agency has considerable scientific expertise and oversees a network of U.S. national laboratories, some of which conduct advanced biological research.
