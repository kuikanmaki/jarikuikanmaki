no history of contact with animals, visits to markets

Patients 1–6 had met with their relatives (relatives 2–5: one female cousin and three aunts of patient 3) every day during their stay in Wuhan for meals

Patients 1–5 transmitted the virus to patient 7 on returning to Shenzhen.

Attack rate is rather high, up to 83%

close contacts with relatives (1-5)

patients 3–6 stayed with patient 7
