Scientists have found three viruses in bats in Laos that are more similar to SARS-CoV-2 than any known viruses. Researchers say that parts of their genetic code bolster claims that the virus behind COVID-19 has [a natural origin](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-021-02519-1) — but their discovery also raises fears that there are numerous coronaviruses with the potential to infect people.
