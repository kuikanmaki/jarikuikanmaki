Key Technical Points

Furin cleavage site. SARS-CoV-2 remains the only known bat-borne sarbecovirus with a furin cleavage site in its spike protein, see Ebright, 2024: “SARS-CoV-2 is the only one of more than 800 known SARS viruses that possesses a furin cleavage site.” Intermediate host. No intermediate host for SARS-CoV-2 has ever been found (Alwine, James, et al., A Critical Analysis of the Evidence for the SARS-CoV-2 Origin Hypotheses, Journal of Virology, March 28, 2023, Chapter 16 WHO SAGO 2025). Patient Zero. Identity of Patient Zero remains officially unknown, with genome analysis ruling out the earliest known cases as the first infection (see: Chapter 17 Sources and Notes). While various theories have emerged, including claims of the first case being a researcher at the Wuhan Institute of Virology, no direct, independently verifiable evidence has been produced on the identity of Patient Zero.
