Notes to Chapter 4


Notes on Sources 2 and 13

Later research indicated that SARS-CoV-2 may have already been circulating earlier than first thought in the countries mentioned. For evidence of earlier-than-reported spread in France, see Chapter 17 Deslandes, A. et al. 2020. For evidence of earlier-than-reported spread in Italy, see Chapter 17 La Rosa et al 2020 and Apolone et al 2020.


Cross-References

The significance of RaTG13 (the closest known relative of SARS-CoV-2) and the unusual furin cleavage site (FCS) in the virus’s spike protein are discussed in detail in many later sections of the book, see especially Chapters 5, 11, 12, 14 and 19.
