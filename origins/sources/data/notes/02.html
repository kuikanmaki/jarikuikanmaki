Notes to Chapter 2


Note on Source 1

The Washington Times article was originally published on January 24, 2020, under the headline “Virus-hit Wuhan has two laboratories linked to Chinese bio-warfare program.” For more on Dany Shoham’s earlier research into Chinese biological warfare programs: Shoham, Dany, China’s Biological Warfare Programme, Journal of Defence Studies, April-June 2015; for his later views on the COVID-19 pandemic: Shoham, Dany, The Roots of the COVID-19 Pandemic, BESA Center, December 14, 2020.


Further Reading

On the early history of US and Japanese biowarfare programs, human experimentation, and their links to later covert operations (including Operation Paperclip, Unit 731, Operation Seaspray, Fort Detrick, and MKUltra): Kinzer, Stephen, Poisoner in Chief: Sidney Gottlieb and the CIA Search for Mind Control, Henry Holt and Co., 2019. On lab leaks: Furmanski, Martin, A brief, terrifying history of viruses escaping from labs: 70s Chinese pandemic was a lab mistake, National Post, April 11, 2014. The final report and supporting volumes of the Church Committee (US Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities) are publicly available online.
