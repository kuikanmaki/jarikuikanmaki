Note on Sources 4 to 7: Media Reporting of the Lab Leak Theory

Several outlets and analysts examined how the lab-leak hypothesis was dismissed by the media: Jenkins Jr., Holman W., Wuhan Lab Theory Is a Media Warning, Wall Street Journal, May 28, 2021, Rozado, David, Prevalence in News Media of Two Competing Hypotheses about COVID-19 Origins, Social Sciences, August 24, 2021, Rindsberg, Ashley, The Lab Leak Fiasco, Tablet Magazine, November 16, 2021.


Fact-Checking Reversals

For example, in September 2020 the fact-checking service PolitiFact declared lab-origin claims false, stating that “the genetic structure of the novel coronavirus … rules out the possibility that it was manipulated in a lab.” In May 2021, the outlet added an editor’s note acknowledging that the question remained under scientific debate: Tucker Carlson guest airs debunked conspiracy theory that coronavirus was created in a lab, PolitiFact, Editor’s Note, May 17, 2021.
