Notes to Chapter 1

Naming of SARS-CoV-2

The virus was initially designated 2019-nCoV (novel coronavirus 2019). It received its official name, SARS-CoV-2, on 11 February, 2020.

Further Reading

Additional early Western media coverage that explicitly mentioned the Wuhan BSL-4 laboratory in connection with the outbreak: Rahhal, Natalie, Wuhan, China's coronavirus epicentre, has SARS and Ebola lab, Daily Mail, January 23, 2020.
